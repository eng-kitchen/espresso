Espresso

Keep your Mac awake.

A tiny menu bar app that prevents your Mac from sleeping while you work. Toggle on demand or set a timer up to 5 hours. No dock icon. No Apple tax.

Install

Brew it in Terminal

One line installs to ~/Applications, ad-hoc signs the app, and clears Gatekeeper. This is how you skip “unidentified developer” without a paid Apple ID. Read the recipe.

curl -fsSL https://eng-kitchen.github.io/espresso/install.sh | bash
